Juggernaut suit sounds

Footsteps: the game's own footstep sound for the current surface, replayed heavier by the plugin
(pitched down, high frequencies cut), with a synthesized low impact layer (tools/sfx/build_sfx.py).
Servos, landing and the power motor are synthesized in tools/sfx/build_sfx.py.

CC0 / public domain sources used for the remaining layers (from OpenGameArt.org):
- "Quick Drill Fix" (roboticsqueeze) by qubodup - CC0 (jump servo)
  https://opengameart.org/content/quick-drill-fix
- "Mechanical Sounds" by BMacZero - CC0 (jump clank, creaks, power latch)
  https://opengameart.org/content/mechanical-sounds
